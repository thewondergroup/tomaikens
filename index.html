Every dish here began as a memory.a morning baking with my mother.fruit picked with my brother Robert.a bottle my father opened.shellfish landing in a Paris kitchen.two stars at twenty-six.wild grouse from the Pennine moors.

A converted mews house in Belgravia, twenty-five seats, and a menu written from the memories that made Tom Aikens a chef.

Tom Aikens

Back at the pass, every service.

Trained under Pierre Koffmann and Joël Robuchon, Tom earned his second star at twenty-six. At Muse he is back at the pass every service, cooking a menu drawn from his own life — and telling you why.

Tom's story
The house

A Georgian mews, with room for twenty-five.

Muse opened in January 2020 and earned its Michelin star within the year. Two floors, an open kitchen, and a dining room that feels closer to a dinner party than a restaurant.

Tom Aikens

Raised in Norfolk. Trained in the hardest kitchens in Europe.

David Cavalier's, La Tante Claire, Pied-à-Terre, Robuchon in Paris. Muse is his return to cooking for a room small enough to know every guest.

“My mother taught me to bake when I was eight. My father taught me that a bottle of wine could be a map of somewhere. Everything else followed.”

Tom's story and the team
Provenance

We cook with people who care as much as we do.

Almost everything at Muse comes from British farmers, fishermen, millers and makers we know by name. Many have supplied the kitchen since the day it opened.
