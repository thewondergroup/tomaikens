Privacy and terms.

How we handle your information when you book, write to us or use this site.

Privacy

Who we are. This website is operated by Muse by Tom Aikens, 38 Groom Place, London SW1X 7BA. You can reach us at info@musebytomaikens.co.uk.

What we collect. When you book a table through SevenRooms, buy a voucher through Vouchable, email us, or join our newsletter, we receive the details you give — typically your name, contact details, the date and size of your booking, and any dietary or access requirements. This site sets no tracking cookies of its own.

How we use it. To take and manage your reservation, to contact you about it, to meet dietary and access needs, and — only where you have asked for it — to send news of menus and events. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Who we share it with. Our reservation, voucher and email providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms: SevenRooms (reservations), Vouchable (vouchers) and our email platform (newsletter). We share nothing else except where the law requires.

How long we keep it. Booking records for as long as we need them for accounting and guest history; newsletter details until you unsubscribe, which you can do from any email.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@musebytomaikens.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Terms of use

Menus and prices are a guide to the season and may change without notice. Links to other sites are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for their content.
