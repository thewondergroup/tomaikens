What's on

Nights that only happen once.

The Culinary Creators series brings some of Britain's most acclaimed chefs to the counter for a single evening alongside Tom. Then there is the cellar, which opens every Tuesday.

Coming up
Culinary Creators · 13 January

Tom Aikens × Jackson Boxer

Eight courses, cooked live at the counter and split evenly between the two chefs. Jackson Boxer is the self-taught chef-restaurateur behind Brunswick House in Vauxhall, Orasay and now Dove in Notting Hill, and Vesper on Exmouth Market — produce-first, seasonal, and with an eye for a room.

£225per personReserve a seat
Wine Cellar Series · Every Tuesday

An evening built around the cellar.

Each Tuesday, Tom and head sommelier Elisa Marchini pair wines to the evening's seasonal dishes. The opening run is devoted to the Languedoc — warm-climate Grenache, Syrah and Carignan — with new regions to follow. Small numbers, around the counter.

£65wine pairing, per person, booked in advanceBook a Tuesday
The Culinary Creators series

Two chefs, eight dishes, one night.

A run of one-off evenings with the chefs and hospitality figures shaping the industry now — from classical fine-dining veterans to cooks who came up outside it. Tom and the guest cook eight dishes between them, live, in a room relaxed enough to watch it happen.

Menus are written for each night and released closer to the date. To hear about new dates first, join the newsletter at the foot of the page.

Women in Wine · 14 October

Four winemakers, four estates, one table.

An evening of wine with food from the kitchen, the makers in the room to talk through their estates and the thinking behind each pour.

Beatriz Cabral Almeida

Quinta dos Carvalhais, Dão, Portugal — a winemaker who came to the cellar through microbiology.

Sofia Ricasoli

Fifth generation of the Ricasoli family, and founder of Innesto, making her own Tuscan wines.

Sophie Cossy

Took over her family's Champagne house in the Montagne de Reims at twenty-five; vines there since 1764.

Laura Iverson

Partner and Director at Chêne Bleu, a Provence estate restored from a medieval priory.
